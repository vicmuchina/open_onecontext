OpenContext Law Policy (editable)

Purpose
- This text policy is passed to the watchman model with session evidence.
- Keep rules explicit, short, and testable.

Authoring Rules
- Describe trigger + required behavior + expected tools/commands.
- Mention concrete tool/skill names when needed.
- Keep one rule per line or short numbered block.

Example Rules
1) For long-running terminal tasks (dev/watch/repl), use PTY/background tooling instead of blocking bash.
2) If relevant MCP tooling exists for docs/research, use MCP first.
3) When docs/GitHub research changes implementation, checkpoint with opencontext commit.
4) Before retrying after failures, inspect prior attempts with opencontext context --search/--log.
